## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an authoritative, accessible, and dignified civic administrative environment for municipal governance. Tailored specifically for municipal archival, incoming/outgoing correspondence tracking, and official dispatch ("Bureau d'Ordre"), the interface balances the solemnity of public institutional service with modern operational efficiency. 

The aesthetic is **Civic Corporate Modern**, drawing on formal Moroccan administrative traditions through architectural balance, subtle geometric discipline, and deliberate high-readability visual density. It strips away ornamental distractions in favor of crisp administrative clarity, deliberate contrast, and calm utilitarian prestige.

### Core Character
- **Institutional Dignity:** Reflects the authority of state records without feeling antiquated or bureaucratic.
- **Precision & Efficiency:** Optimized for intensive document indexing, rapid data classification, status verification, and high-throughput scanning pipelines.
- **Bilingual Civic Legibility:** Prioritizes right-to-left (RTL) Arabic typography as the first-class layout direction, with parallel French indexing accommodated seamlessly.
- **Reassurance & Trust:** Solid, grounded surfaces and controlled visual weights evoke permanency, discretion, and compliance.

## Colors

The palette is anchored by the deep forestry of Moroccan institutional green, counterpointed by regal amber-gold accents that highlight certification, executive actions, and municipal insignia. 

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary (`#0D4A36`):** Forest Sovereign Green. Serves as the bedrock brand color for application toolbars, primary call-to-actions, top-level navigation states, and confirmed administrative actions.
- **Secondary (`#C99738`):** Warm Royal Ochre / Gold. Reserved for municipal insignias, VIP correspondence tags, pinned decrees, tertiary highlights, and secondary interactive focus accents.
- **Tertiary (`#1D3B53`):** Deep Diplomatic Slate Blue. Used for incoming archival queues, legal registry links, metadata tagging, and official dossiers.
- **Neutrals & Canvas:**
  - `Surface Base`: `#F8FAF9` (Ivory-tinted clean institutional backdrop).
  - `Surface Raised / Card`: `#FFFFFF`.
  - `Surface Recessed / Table Stripe`: `#EDF2EF`.
  - `Border Subtle`: `#DCE4E0`.
  - `Border Strong`: `#B6C5BF`.
  - `Text Primary`: `#132A22` (High-contrast, deep forest-slate tone preventing the harshness of pure black).
  - `Text Muted`: `#4D655D`.

### Semantic Matrix
- **Processed / Approved / Success:** Emerald (`#127A52` text/fill on `#E6F5EF` background).
- **Pending / Under Review / In Progress:** Amber Warmth (`#A26E0B` text/fill on `#FDF5E6` background).
- **Urgent / Delayed / Expired:** Ruby Crimson (`#BA2525` text/fill on `#FDF0F0` background).
- **Incoming / Registered / Archived:** Marine Navy (`#1D476E` text/fill on `#EDF4FA` background).

## Typography

The typographical framework is engineered around native bidirectional support. It utilizes IBM Plex Sans (and its Arabic counterpart IBM Plex Sans Arabic), which marries rationalized technical discipline with authentic Arabic calligraphic foundations (Naskh structure with modern upright posture).

### Application Rules
- **Bidirectional Symmetry:** Typography must seamlessly align to the right in Arabic mode (`dir="rtl"`) and to the left in French administrative mode (`dir="ltr"`).
- **Line Heights:** Arabic glyphs possess taller ascenders and deeper descenders than Latin characters. Consequently, line-height tokens are calibrated with generous heights (+4px to +6px higher than standard Western body typography) to eliminate clipping of diacritics and hamzas.
- **Reference Numbers & Slugs:** File reference numbers (e.g., `BO-2024-00891/SG`) use the dedicated `numeric-registry` token featuring tabular, proportional lining figures to align precisely in multi-line tracking ledgers.

## Layout & Spacing

The design system implements a strict 8pt grid with 4pt half-steps for micro-alignments, suited for high-density document dashboards and multi-field government forms.

### Layout Philosophy
- **Workspace Canvas:** Fixed-fluid hybrid layout. On large screens (1440px and above), dense master-detail consoles can expand up to a structured 1680px maximum container width, preserving peripheral margins while maximizing table data visibility without horizontal scrolling.
- **Directional Properties:** Margin and padding tokens must always map to logical CSS properties (`padding-inline-start`, `margin-inline-end`) rather than physical left/right rules, guaranteeing instant, error-free RTL/LTR mirroring.
- **Breakpoints:**
  - **Mobile (`< 640px`):** 4-column structure. Toolbars collapse to sticky bottom action trays. Cards display vertically stacked registry fields.
  - **Tablet (`640px – 1024px`):** 8-column layout. Split views switch to tabbed views or overlay drawers.
  - **Desktop (`> 1024px`):** 12-column structure with a persistent right-aligned (in RTL) administrative navigation drawer, dedicated utility header, and wide multi-column ledger view.

## Elevation & Depth

To maintain administrative authority and pristine legibility under ambient office lighting, elevation avoids aggressive dramatization or floating neomorphic illusions. The system relies on **Tonal Stratification** paired with **Crisp Ambient Shadows**.

### Elevation Scale
1. **Level 0 (Base Canvas):** Background tone `#F8FAF9`. Purely planar, non-interactive.
2. **Level 1 (Cards, Form Panels, Table Containers):** Surface `#FFFFFF`, reinforced with a structural hairline outline (`1px solid #DCE4E0`) and an imperceptible diffused tint:  
   `box-shadow: 0 1px 3px 0 rgba(13, 74, 54, 0.04), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(13, 74, 54, 0.02)`.
3. **Level 2 (Hovered Records, Active Floating Bars, Dropdown Menus):** Surface `#FFFFFF`, border `#B6C5BF`:  
   `box-shadow: 0 4px 6px -1px rgba(13, 74, 54, 0.07), 0 2px 4px -2px rgba(13, 74, 54, 0.05)`.
4. **Level 3 (Modals, Citizen Dispatch Dossiers, Command Palettes):** Surface `#FFFFFF`, layered with a backdrop blur overlay (`backdrop-filter: blur(4px); background-color: rgba(19, 42, 34, 0.40)`):  
   `box-shadow: 0 20px 25px -5px rgba(13, 74, 54, 0.12), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(13, 74, 54, 0.08)`.

### Border Accentuation
Elevation is frequently communicated through structural borders rather than heavy drop shadows, reinforcing the look and feel of authoritative legal ledgers and bound registries.

## Shapes

The interface embraces a tailored **Level 2 Roundedness** architecture that imparts an approachable yet structured character.

### Radii Hierarchy
- **Micro Radii (`rounded-sm` / 4px):** Form checkboxes, radio focus rings, and inline code / registry stamps.
- **Component Radii (`rounded` / 8px):** Form input fields, standard buttons, dropdown lists, search bars, and action menus.
- **Card & Container Radii (`rounded-lg` / 14px - 16px):** Standard administrative cards, document metadata widgets, and data tables.
- **Overlay & Modal Radii (`rounded-xl` / 20px):** Administrative dialog modals, full-page sheets, and media preview wrappers.
- **Full Radii (`rounded-full` / 9999px):** Status indicators, urgency pills, user avatars, and counter badges.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** High-weight solid `#0D4A36` with `#FFFFFF` text. Hover transitions to `#083325`. Focus rings provide a 2px offset in `#C99738` (Amber Gold).
- **Secondary (Institutional):** Crisp `#FFFFFF` surface with `1.5px solid #0D4A36` outline and `#0D4A36` typography.
- **Tertiary / Ghost:** Ghost canvas with `#132A22` text, shifting to a subtle `#EDF2EF` fill on hover.
- **Urgent / Destructive:** Crimson `#BA2525` background or ghost variant with red boundary for expunging or voiding registry files.
- **Sizing:** Standard touch targets at 40px height for dense desktop data entry, 48px for mobile.

### Badges & Status Pills
- Always styled as full pills (`rounded-full`) with an internal dot indicator (6px circle).
- Font size is locked to `label-sm` (11px) with bold weight.
- **Status Types:**
  - *Processed (معالج / Traité):* `#E6F5EF` fill, `#127A52` text, emerald dot.
  - *Pending (قيد المعالجة / En cours):* `#FDF5E6` fill, `#A26E0B` text, amber dot.
  - *Critical / High Urgency (عاجل / Urgent):* `#FDF0F0` fill, `#BA2525` text, crimson blinking/solid dot.
  - *Incoming Registered (وارد / Arrivée):* `#EDF4FA` fill, `#1D476E` text, navy dot.

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Text Inputs & Dropdowns:** 40px height, `#FFFFFF` fill with 1px `#DCE4E0` border. Active focus transitions border to `#0D4A36` with a faint `0 0 0 3px rgba(13, 74, 54, 0.12)` glow.
- **Labels:** Set in `label-md`, anchored to the right in RTL context, with red asterisk (`#BA2525`) for mandatory fields.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Colored in `#0D4A36` when selected, with high-contrast white check icon.

### Data Tables (The Core Ledger)
- **Container:** Rounded (`14px`), framed in a structural `1px solid #DCE4E0` perimeter border.
- **Header:** Sticky header locked to `#F8FAF9`, featuring uppercase/bold `label-md` muted green text (`#4D655D`), divided by a 2px border underneath (`#B6C5BF`).
- **Row Architecture:** Alternate row zebra striping using `#FFFFFF` and `#FAFBFB`. Row height is set to an efficient 48px.
- **Hover State:** Rows transition to `#F0F5F2` on hover, revealing quick-action icons (view, route, print receipt, download PDF) on the row's physical terminal side.

### Cards & Dossier Panels
- White background (`#FFFFFF`) with 14px–16px border-radius.
- Cards are partitioned by a subtle 1px divider (`#EDF2EF`) separating the formal header (containing the dossier title, barcode/QR stamp, and urgency pill) from the body payload (correspondence subject, sender entity, date of receipt, and attached official stamps).
